Stay Safe When Applying

Legitimate government and municipal job applications should not require payment to secure a job or interview. Never pay a person who promises you an appointment. Verify vacancies through the official employer website before sharing personal information.

Privacy Notice

Your information and privacy

JobLink AI stores your CV details and job application records in your browser on your device. This information is not automatically synchronised across devices or uploaded to a JobLink AI account.

  • Your information: People with access to your device or browser may be able to view saved information.
  • Browser data: Clearing browser data may remove saved CV details and applications. Keep separate copies of important documents.
  • Job searches: When you open an external job link, that website's privacy policy and terms apply.
  • Check your CV: Review your information before downloading, printing or sharing your CV.
  • Job listings: JobLink AI does not guarantee employment or independently verify every vacancy.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for your job application.
